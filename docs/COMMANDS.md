# Twitch chat commands

Only joined viewers control avatars or display speech bubbles. Commands are matched by Twitch user ID, not display name.

## Everyone

- `!join` — join the world; repeated joins do not duplicate an active avatar.
- `!build <description>` — while Community Sandbox is active, request a miniature voxel creation. The streamer must pick up your monster within 15 seconds to approve it.
- `!blast` — fire a chat projectile during the Streamer vs. Chat challenge.
- `!support`, `!like`, `!fire` — community reactions.
- `!leave` — release and remove the viewer avatar promptly, even while held.

Normal non-command chat appears temporarily in a speech bubble above the sender's joined avatar.

## Big monsters

`!run` · `!jump` · `!wave` · `!duck` · `!punch` · `!yes` · `!no`

## Blob monsters

`!dance` · `!jump` · `!bite` · `!walk` · `!yes` · `!no`

## Flying monsters

`!fly` · `!headbutt` · `!punch` · `!yes` · `!no`

Unsupported commands are ignored. A viewer who leaves and rejoins during the same server session receives the next monster in the six-character rotation.
